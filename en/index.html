01 / SCOPE

What does this policy cover?

This policy applies to this website and to mobile games published by Mostly Functional Studios that explicitly link to this policy and use Google AdMob with Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).

Different games may offer additional features or use additional providers. Where applicable, such processing must be described here or in a game-specific notice. Without further additions, this policy does not cover user accounts, cloud saves, in-app purchases, Firebase Analytics or separate web advertising services.

03 / WEBSITE

This website & GitHub Pages

This static site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, the hosting provider may process technical connection information including your IP address, time of access, requested URL, and browser and device information. This helps deliver the website and maintain its security and availability.

We do not add tracking scripts, analytics services, advertising networks or externally hosted fonts to this website. The hosting provider may process information independently; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Where personal data is involved, our legal basis for providing this website is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in providing a secure, accessible information page).

04 / GAMEPLAY

Games & local storage

Depending on the game, progress, high scores, preferences and other information needed to play may be stored locally on your device. Such data is not necessarily sent to us. Whether a game sends data elsewhere depends on the features and services it actually uses.

Where the information is personal data and processing is necessary to deliver gameplay requested by you, the applicable legal basis may be Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

05 / ADVERTISING

Google AdMob

Our designated mobile games use Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google (generally Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, for users in the EEA). Advertising helps fund our games.

Depending on the device, region, settings and consent choices, Google and participating ad technology providers may process the following information to deliver, limit, measure and protect ads:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it,
  • device and app information (such as operating system, app version and device model),
  • advertising identifiers or other technical identifiers, where available and permitted,
  • ad impressions, interactions and technical diagnostic events.

Where consent is legally required, particularly for personalized advertising or non-essential access to information on your device, we rely on consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and applicable device-access rules. Other processing may, where legally appropriate, rely on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; this depends on the particular purpose.

Specific vendors and purposes are shown in the relevant consent interface. For further information see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner sites and apps.

06 / CONSENT

Consent choices & withdrawal

Where consent is required, our games use Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage privacy choices. Available options depend on your location and applicable requirements.

You may withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. Where a privacy-options entry point is required under UMP, the relevant game provides an option under Settings → Privacy options to revisit your choices. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out lawfully before withdrawal.

Providing consent choices and recording them may involve technical information about your selection. Google provides additional details in its consent interface.

07 / TRANSFERS

Processing outside the EU / EEA

GitHub Pages and Google services may involve the processing of personal data outside the EU or EEA, including in the United States. Depending on the recipient and transfer route, an adequacy decision or safeguards such as standard contractual clauses may apply. Please consult the providers' privacy information for their specific transfer arrangements.

08 / RETENTION

Storage & deletion

Locally stored game progress and settings can generally be removed using the device's or app's storage-management options; the exact options depend on the operating system and the game. If you contact us, we retain your message only as long as required to respond and to comply with any applicable legal obligations.

Where Google or GitHub independently control personal data, their own retention and deletion policies apply. You can request information or deletion of data we control using the contact details above.

09 / YOUR RIGHTS

Your privacy rights

Where the GDPR applies, and subject to applicable requirements, you may have the following rights:

  • access to personal data (Article 15 GDPR),
  • correction of inaccurate data (Article 16),
  • erasure and restriction of processing (Articles 17 and 18),
  • data portability (Article 20),
  • objection to certain processing (Article 21),
  • withdrawal of consent for the future (Article 7(3)).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority (Article 77 GDPR).
